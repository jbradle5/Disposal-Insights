West Coast Region

Sawtooth Regional Landfill reopens in Elmore County

Republic Services reopened the 300-acre Sawtooth Regional Landfill in Elmore County, Idaho, following extensive site upgrades and new cell construction to handle growing regional waste needs. The reopened facility is expected to lower long-term transportation costs for local residents and offers an estimated 200-year operational lifespan.

Facility Reopening

Teamsters workers authorize strike at Republic Services Arizona landfill

Workers represented by Teamsters Local 104 at Republic Services' Southwest Regional Landfill in Buckeye, Arizona, voted unanimously to authorize a strike following stalled contract negotiations regarding wages, benefits, and seniority rules. The union is taking an aggressive bargaining stance backed by an enhanced strike fund.

Republic Strike

San Joaquin County Announces Long-Term Closure of Foothill Sanitary Landfill

San Joaquin County has officially closed the Foothill Sanitary Landfill and consolidated waste operations at the expanded North County Recycling Center and Sanitary Landfill. This strategic consolidation is projected to save the county $80 million over 15 years and has already enabled a 12% to 18% reduction in residential rates.

Facility Closure

Chiquita starts repairs, residents cry foul

Elected officials and local residents are demanding enhanced community protections and temporary housing assistance from Waste Connections as construction begins on the West Toe Buttress Project at Chiquita Canyon Landfill. The stabilization project aims to prevent slope failure caused by an ongoing subsurface thermal reaction.

Ohio EPA cites Carbon Limestone Landfill for more violations

The Ohio EPA issued additional environmental citations to Republic Services' Carbon Limestone Landfill in Poland Township following multiple leachate spills into an on-site stormwater pond and failure to conduct required quarterly assessments.

Waste Connections has refiled its application with New York State for approval to expand the height, footprint, and lifespan of the Seneca Meadows Landfill, which handles nearly 20% of the state's landfilled waste.

Environmental concerns over Twin Creeks landfill expansion

Warwick Township has raised significant environmental concerns over Waste Management Canada's proposed 14-million-cubic-metre vertical expansion of the Twin Creeks Landfill, which would double the site's height to 24 storeys.

Dane County breaks ground on new landfill and sustainability campus

Dane County officials broke ground on a new landfill and sustainability campus off Highway 12 in Madison, Wisconsin, with the landfill slated to open in summer 2027. Funded entirely through enterprise revenues rather than tax dollars.

New Landfill

Operators of Terrebonne, Que., site also seeking to expand landfill's footprint

The Quebec government published an emergency decree granting a 12-month temporary vertical expansion to Complexe Enviro Connexions' landfill in Terrebonne, allowing it to take in 1.2 million additional tonnes of waste.

Essential expansion - Atlantic County Utilities Authority

The Atlantic County Utilities Authority secured approval for a landfill expansion at its Egg Harbor Township facility in New Jersey. The expansion provides long-term waste disposal capacity and helps stabilize controlled waste rates.

Casella Waste withdraws wetland permit for controversial Dalton landfill

Casella Waste Systems withdrew its wetland permit application for the proposed Granite State Landfill near Forest Lake State Park in Dalton, New Hampshire, to ensure proper alignment with its ongoing solid waste permit appeal.

District Court orders Republic Services to pay $9.2 million for Clean Water Act violations

A federal district court ordered Republic Services of Pennsylvania to pay a $9.2 million civil penalty for discharging over 156 million gallons of inadequately treated leachate from Modern Landfill into Kreutz Creek over four years.
