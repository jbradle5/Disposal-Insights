West Tier News

Waste Connections Sues Los Angeles County Over Chiquita Canyon Closure

A Waste Connections subsidiary has filed an $825 million lawsuit against Los Angeles County, alleging that regulatory agencies improperly denied operating and cell permits and forced the Chiquita Canyon Landfill to close amid an ongoing elevated temperature event. While the operator reports the reaction is stabilizing and anticipates spending up to $150 million on site remediation this year, local authorities and residents continue to pursue legal remedies over persistent odors and environmental impacts.

Closure Litigation

Clover Flat Landfill Closure Timeline Slows in Napa County

Waste Connections has delayed submitting a formal closure plan for the Clover Flat Landfill near Calistoga, California, postponing an anticipated capping and bidding schedule previously projected to commence by 2027. The operator intends to shutter the facility strictly for economic reasons and redirect incoming municipal waste to its Potrero Hills Landfill in neighboring Solano County.

Closure Delay

Tillamook County Evaluates Long-Term Solid Waste Disposal Strategies

Tillamook County leaders are collaborating with an Oregon legislative task force to identify alternative disposal solutions for 44,000 annual tons of municipal solid waste as the regional Coffin Butte Landfill nears exhaustion. Local officials favor establishing a state-funded waste partnership over creating a new regional authority to finance intermediary transfer stations and long-haul rail transport to Arlington Landfill.

Capacity Planning

City of Amarillo Landfill Implements New Operating Hours Following Wildfire

The City of Amarillo Landfill in Texas has adopted new year-round operating hours from 8:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. Monday through Saturday after an escaped landfill fire destroyed dozens of neighboring homes. The operational overhaul follows the termination of the facility's superintendent, an independent operational review, and a state environmental violation for inadequate waste covering.

East Tier News

Heart of Florida Landfill Operator Responds to State Warning Letter

Operator A.C.M.S., Inc. has formally replied to a Florida Department of Environmental Protection warning letter that alleged improper dumping methods, toxic leaks, understaffing, and reporting failures at the Heart of Florida landfill in Bushnell. The company maintains that all regulatory matters have been resolved, even as local residents continue to protest proposed test drilling for a 3,200-foot deep injection well to dispose of leachate runoff.

Regulatory Compliance

Missouri Department of Natural Resources Adjusts Solid Waste Tonnage Fees

Beginning October 1, the Missouri Department of Natural Resources will adjust solid waste tonnage fees for sanitary, demolition, and construction landfills based on changes in the Consumer Price Index. The adjustment will generate state funding for illegal dumping enforcement, facility permitting activities, and solid waste grant administration.

Fee Adjustment

WM Reassesses Expansion Plans for Grand Central Landfill in Pennsylvania

WM is revising its development strategy for the Grand Central Landfill in Plainfield Township, Pennsylvania, after the local board of supervisors passed two ordinances restricting expansion onto adjacent farmland. The active site currently receives approximately 2,750 tons of waste per day under a state operating permit capped at 3,000 daily tons.

Expansion Restriction

Maine Excludes Casella Subsidiary as Co-Applicant on Juniper Ridge Expansion

The Maine Department of Environmental Protection has rejected a request for Casella subsidiary NEWSME Landfill Operations to be a co-applicant on the Juniper Ridge Landfill expansion, leaving the state's Bureau of General Services as the sole licensee. The proposed 61-acre expansion would provide 11 years of additional disposal capacity for a facility that Casella projects could reach capacity by 2028 without approval.

Non-Landfill News (Incinerators, Processing, & Logistics)

Labor Strike Halts Operations at Metro Vancouver Waste-to-Energy Incinerator

A strike by nearly 40 Unifor Local 433 workers has forced a temporary closure and operational pause at the Metro Vancouver mass burn combustion facility in Burnaby, British Columbia. The labor stoppage follows stalled contract negotiations with Veolia North America, which took over management of the publicly owned facility in March 2025.

Forced Closure

Clean Harbors Announces $470 Million Acquisition of EnviroServe

Clean Harbors has entered into an agreement to acquire environmental services provider EnviroServe from One Rock Capital Partners for $470 million, adding 40 facilities—including 18 ten-day transfer stations—and a 700-vehicle fleet across 48 states. The transaction expands Clean Harbors' hazardous waste handling, emergency response, and railcar cleaning capabilities as part of an aggressive national M&A strategy.

Corporate Acquisition

Basalt Infrastructure Partners Acquires Waste Logistics Provider Mr. Bult’s Inc.

New York-based Basalt Infrastructure Partners LLP has acquired Burnham, Illinois-based Mr. Bult’s Inc. (MBI), a major provider of outsourced municipal solid waste transportation and transfer station management. Basalt plans to utilize MBI’s specialized transportation fleet, maintenance infrastructure, and long-term customer contracts to capture ongoing demand for outsourced waste hauling.

Transportation Acquisition

Queens Civic Advocates Demand Enforcement of Waste-by-Rail Covering Law

Queens community leaders and elected officials are pushing state authorities to enforce a law mandating sealed, hard covers on waste-hauling rail cars following intense odor complaints along Middle Village freight tracks. Although rail carriers state they remain in full regulatory compliance as litigation over the statute continues, local representatives demand immediate shielding to protect neighborhood quality of life.

Rail Enforcement

Daniels Sharpsmart Fined $47,000 for Hazardous Waste Violations in Minnesota

Daniels Sharpsmart has paid a $47,000 penalty to the Minnesota Pollution Control Agency following an investigation that found 132 unpermitted hazardous waste shipments and unauthorized documentation changes at its Shakopee facility. Under the regulatory agreement, the company has committed to halt unpermitted hazardous waste storage, properly route misdirected materials, and comply with state-approved infectious waste management protocols.
